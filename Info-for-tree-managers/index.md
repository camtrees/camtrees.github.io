# {{ page.title }}

**Tree Managers** are CAM staff members or volunteers who prepare chestnut seedlings for
distribution to the individuals who will ultimately plant them.

The primary purpose of this website is to document the methodologies and procedures used
by the CAMTREES Database system. Although Tree Managers perform many important
responsibilities, this section focuses on one specific task: assigning a permanent, unique
TAG-ID to each chestnut seedling and creating its tree tag.
